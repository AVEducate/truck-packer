WHAT IS TRUCK PACKOLOGY?

Truck Packology teaches the logic AV crews use to load a show truck. Build a heavy base at the nose, pack high and tight, leave no gaps that let cases slide, secure every tier, and load in reverse of the venue's load-in order so the first gear needed comes off first.

A truck can be full and still be a bad pack. Every run is scored on space, stability, unload order and securement, and the crew lead explains each call. 30 beginner floor levels, 30 intermediate stacking levels, expert levels, offloading, and a final exam for your AV Educate certificate.
